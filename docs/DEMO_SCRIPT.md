# Demo video plan (about 1 minute)

Two browser windows help: one signed in as the instructor, one (incognito) as the student. Demo logins are in the README.

| Time | What to show | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:07 | Home page, then the login page | "This is LearnSphere, the LMS I built with React, Express, Firebase and Supabase." |
| 0:07 to 0:15 | Sign in as the student, land on the dashboard | "Students sign in with email or Google and see their courses, deadlines and marks here." |
| 0:15 to 0:27 | Open a course, a module, then answer the quiz | "Each course is split into modules. A module with a quiz only counts as done once you pass it." |
| 0:27 to 0:36 | Assignments page, click Turn in and attach a file | "Assignments can be handed in as text, a link or a file, straight from this list." |
| 0:36 to 0:46 | Switch to the instructor, open Submissions and give marks | "The instructor sees everything waiting to be marked and gives marks and feedback." |
| 0:46 to 0:53 | Instructor dashboard charts, then the course editor | "They also get enrollment charts and can build courses, modules and quizzes." |
| 0:53 to 1:00 | Student's certificate page | "When a student finishes a course they get a certificate that anyone can verify online." |
